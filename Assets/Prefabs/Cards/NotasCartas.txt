defence

Control

Curse of Weakness  Enemy does less 25% damage for the next 4 turns

Life Drain  the next attack you do will heal half of the damage dealt
Empowerment  gain 15% damage resist and 15% damage (damage next turn)
Arcane Theft  steal enemy shield for next turn, if no shield, deal a small amount of damage
